Why Scarce

Luxury is a discipline of restraint. Nobody talks about how it’s actually run.

Every brand in this world trades on scarcity — of materials, of time, of access, of attention. Yet the people responsible for growing those brands have to answer to the same forces as everyone else: performance targets, platforms that reward volume, clients who expect to be reached but never sold to.

Scarce is a series of candid, long-form interviews with the marketing and brand leaders who sit in the middle of that tension. How you grow without diluting. How you measure without cheapening. What actually worked, what didn’t, and what you’d tell the person taking over your seat.

Who it’s for

The audience

Founders, co-founders, managing directors and the brand and marketing leads who work alongside them — at consumer luxury brands in jewellery, watches, fashion, travel, property and automotive, and at the B2B businesses that serve high-net-worth clients: private aviation, wealth and family-office services, hospitality, concierge, private members’ clubs.

The guests

People with something worth hearing. Founders, MDs and heads of marketing, alongside the creators, writers, media professionals and advisors who shape how this world is seen. Guests bring first-hand experience: a decision they made, a campaign they ran, a story they told, a market they opened, or a mistake they learned from.

The conversation

One guest, one host, one hour. Specific rather than sweeping. We’d rather spend twenty minutes on how you rebuilt your CRM than two minutes each on ten trends.

How an episode works

You keep control of what goes out with your name on it.

We know a public conversation is a risk for anyone representing a brand with standards to protect. The process is built to remove that risk.

  1. 01

    A short pre-call

    Twenty minutes to find the two or three things you’re best placed to talk about. No preparation needed.

  2. 02

    Questions shared in advance

    You see every question before we record. Strike out, reword, or add anything you like. Nothing is asked that you haven’t seen.

  3. 03

    Recording

    45–60 minutes, remote over a studio-quality link, or in person in London or Barcelona. We handle all the technical side; you need a quiet room and a laptop.

  4. 04

    Your review

    You receive the edited cut before anything is published. Anything you’d rather not have on the record comes out, no questions asked.

  5. 05

    Release

    Full episode in audio and video on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and YouTube. Short clips across LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok — and a set delivered to you for your own channels.

The kind of thing we ask

Specific, generous, and never a trap.

A few examples. Your own list is written with you on the pre-call.

  • What’s the one metric you’re asked to hit that actively works against the brand?
  • Walk us through a campaign that looked wrong on paper and was right in the room.
  • How do you sell a £40,000 piece to someone you’re not allowed to “market” to?
  • What did opening the Middle East (or the US, or Asia) teach you about your own brand at home?
  • Which agency relationship changed how you buy services, and why?
  • What would you tell the person who takes your job in five years?

Questions you might have

The practical bits.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. The pre-call and the shared question list do the preparation. Most guests read the questions once the night before.

Can I decline a question, or take something back afterwards?

Yes to both. Strike anything from the list before we record, and remove anything from the edit before we publish. We’d rather have a slightly shorter episode you’re proud of.

Does my company need to sign off?

Often, yes — and we’re used to it. We’ll send a one-page brief you can share internally, and your comms or legal team is welcome on the review.

Where is it published?

Audio on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and everywhere else podcasts are found. Video on YouTube. Clips on LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok, with a set delivered to you.

How much time does it take?

About two hours in total: a 20-minute pre-call, an hour to record, and however long you want to spend on the review.

Is there a cost?

None. Scarce is not a paid placement and there is no fee for appearing.
